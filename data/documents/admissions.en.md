## Dates and application process {#dates}
Admissions for the academic year 2027-28 open on May 1, 2027 and close on July 31, 2027. The last date to apply is July 31, 2027. Apply through the online admission portal by filling in the application form and uploading your documents. The merit list is published on August 10, 2027, and classes begin on September 1, 2027.

## Eligibility {#eligibility}
B.Sc AI: Class 12 passed with Mathematics and at least 50% marks. BCA: Class 12 passed from a recognized board in any stream. MCA: a bachelor's degree with Mathematics at the 10+2 or graduation level. M.Sc Data Science: a bachelor's degree in Science, Computer Applications or Engineering with at least 55% marks.

## Required documents {#documents}
Bring the Class 10 and Class 12 marksheets, an Aadhaar card, passport-size photographs and the Transfer Certificate. A valid government-issued photo ID is needed during document verification. Scholarship applicants must also submit an income certificate and a category certificate where applicable.

## International students {#international}
International students apply through the International Admissions Office. Applications must reach the office by June 30 so that visa support can be arranged. You need a passport, your previous academic transcripts and an equivalence certificate for your qualification. Write to international@northfield.example for help.
