## Placement statistics {#statistics}
The university maintains an overall placement rate of about 90%. Last year the highest package was 12 LPA and the average package was 5.5 LPA.

## Top recruiters {#recruiters}
Top recruiters include TCS, Infosys, Accenture, Wipro, Capgemini and Cognizant. Around 60 companies visit the campus every year.

## Internships and training {#internships}
The placement cell helps students find internships. Summer internships are done after the fourth semester. Aptitude training and mock interviews start in the fifth semester.
