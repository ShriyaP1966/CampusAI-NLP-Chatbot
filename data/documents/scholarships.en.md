## Merit scholarship {#merit}
Students who scored above 90% in Class 12 receive a 50% tuition waiver. Students with 85% to 90% receive a 25% tuition waiver. The scholarship is renewed every year if the student keeps a CGPA of at least 8.0.

## Government scholarships {#government}
Eligible students may apply for state and central government scholarship schemes through the national scholarship portal. The scholarship cell helps with the forms. Submit the income certificate and category certificate, and apply before October 31.

## Sports scholarship {#sports}
Outstanding athletes who have played at state or national level can receive a tuition waiver of up to 40%. Applicants must attach their participation certificates and attend a selection trial held in July.

## Scholarship for girls {#girls}
Eligible female students whose family income is below 4 lakh rupees per year receive a scholarship of 10,000 rupees every year. Apply with an income certificate at the time of admission.
