## Hostel {#hostel}
Separate hostels are available for boys and girls, with Wi-Fi, a mess, laundry, study rooms and recreation areas. The girls' hostel has 24x7 security and CCTV surveillance. Apply for hostel accommodation during the admission process.

## Library {#library}
The library holds over 50,000 books along with digital resources and journals. It is open from 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM, Monday to Saturday, and stays open round the clock during examination weeks.

## Wi-Fi and laboratories {#wifi-labs}
Free high-speed Wi-Fi is available across the campus. The AI and computer science laboratories have high-performance computers with GPUs and are open from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM.

## Transport {#transport}
Bus transportation runs from major parts of the city to the campus. The annual bus fee is 18,000 rupees, and bus passes are issued by the transport office.

## Medical centre {#medical}
The campus medical centre provides first aid and a doctor from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM. An ambulance is available around the clock for emergencies.

## Cafeteria {#cafeteria}
The campus cafeteria serves hygienic meals and refreshments from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM, every day except Sunday.

## Student clubs {#clubs}
Students can join technical, cultural, sports and entrepreneurship clubs. Clubs recruit new members at the Club Fair held in the second week of August.
