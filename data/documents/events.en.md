## Freshers orientation {#orientation}
The orientation programme for new students runs from September 1 to September 3, 2027. It includes campus tours, a meeting with faculty mentors and a session on student services.

## Annual sports day {#sports-day}
The Annual Sports Day takes place on January 12, 2028 at the main ground. It includes athletics, cricket, football and inter-department tournaments.

## Northfield TechFest {#techfest}
Northfield TechFest, the annual technical festival, is held from February 18 to February 20, 2028. It features a 24-hour hackathon, workshops on AI and robotics and a paper presentation contest.

## Cultural festival {#cultural-fest}
The cultural festival, Rangotsav, is held on March 10 and 11, 2028. It includes music, dance, drama and an art exhibition.

## Convocation {#convocation}
The convocation ceremony is on March 25, 2028. Graduating students must apply for the degree certificate by January 31, 2028 through the exam portal.
