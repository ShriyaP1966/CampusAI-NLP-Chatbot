## Programmes offered {#programmes}
The university offers undergraduate and postgraduate programmes in Science, Commerce, Management and Technology. B.Sc Artificial Intelligence is a 3-year programme covering AI, machine learning, data science and NLP. BCA is a 3-year programme covering programming, databases, networking and software development. MCA is a 2-year postgraduate programme focused on advanced software development and cloud computing. M.Sc Data Science is a 2-year programme covering statistics, machine learning, big data and visualization.

## Seats {#seats}
The intake for 2027-28 is 60 seats in B.Sc AI, 120 seats in BCA, 60 seats in MCA and 40 seats in M.Sc Data Science. Seats are filled strictly by the published merit list.

## Curriculum structure {#curriculum}
Undergraduate programmes run for six semesters and postgraduate programmes for four semesters. Every student takes core subjects, two electives and a final-year project. Each semester carries about 22 credits, and the project is guided by a faculty mentor.
