## Tuition fees {#tuition}
The annual tuition fee is 85,000 rupees for B.Sc AI, 70,000 rupees for BCA, 90,000 rupees for MCA and 95,000 rupees for M.Sc Data Science. These amounts do not include hostel or bus charges.

## Hostel and mess charges {#hostel}
The hostel fee is 45,000 rupees per academic year including accommodation. The optional mess plan costs 30,000 rupees per year. A refundable security deposit of 5,000 rupees is collected at check-in.

## Payment methods and late fee {#payment}
Fees can be paid online through UPI, net banking, debit card or credit card. Tuition may be paid in two instalments: 60% at admission and 40% by January 15. A late fee of 100 rupees per day applies after the due date, up to a maximum of 2,000 rupees.

## Refund policy {#refunds}
If you withdraw before August 31, you receive a full refund minus a 5,000 rupee processing charge. Between September 1 and September 30 the refund is 50% of the tuition fee. No refund is given after September 30.
