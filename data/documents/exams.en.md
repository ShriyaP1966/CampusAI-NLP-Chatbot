## Exam schedule {#schedule}
Odd-semester examinations are held from December 1 to December 20 and even-semester examinations from May 10 to May 30. The detailed timetable is published on the exam portal three weeks before each examination.

## Hall ticket and attendance {#hall-ticket}
Hall tickets can be downloaded from the exam portal seven days before the first paper. Students need at least 75% attendance to sit the examinations. Attendance between 65% and 75% can be condoned with a medical certificate approved by the head of department.

## Grading and passing marks {#grading}
Results use a 10-point CGPA scale. A student must score at least 40% in every paper to pass. A grade of O is awarded for 90% and above, and the minimum CGPA to graduate is 4.0.

## Revaluation and supplementary exams {#revaluation}
Apply for revaluation within seven days of the result announcement. The fee is 500 rupees per paper and the revised result comes within 15 days. Students with up to three backlog papers can take supplementary examinations in July.
