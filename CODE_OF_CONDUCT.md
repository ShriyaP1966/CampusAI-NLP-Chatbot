# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a respectful and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, background, gender identity and expression, experience level, nationality, race, religion or sexual orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to NLP, Python or open source.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Assume good intent, and ask when something is unclear.
- Keep discussion focused on the project.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks.
- Publishing other people's private information.
- Sexualised language or imagery.
- Spam or unrelated promotion.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and pull requests that do not follow this code of conduct, and may temporarily or permanently block anyone whose behaviour they consider inappropriate.

To report a problem, use GitHub's "Report content" option on the comment or contact the maintainer through the email address on their GitHub profile. Reports are handled privately.
